The Brick family in one paragraph

The Brick (TG3040), Brick Hammer and Brick Pro (TG4040) share TrimUI's Allwinner A133P platform with the Smart Pro (TG5040): four Cortex-A53 cores, a PowerVR GE8300 GPU, 1 GB of RAM, an aarch64 glibc userland on a 4.9 vendor kernel, and a 1024×768 screen [tbr-hw] [handkit-platforms]. NextUI calls this platform tg5040 and ships one build for all of them [nextui-paks]. The Smart Pro S (TG5050) is a different chip and needs its own builds.
